# Digital Resources and E-Books

- Riverbend Community Library offers e-books and audiobooks through its
  digital lending app, available to all members with a valid library card.
- Digital loans follow a 2-week loan period and are returned
  automatically - there are no late fees on digital items.
- Standard members may borrow up to 3 digital items at a time; Premium
  members may borrow up to 10.
- The library also provides free access to several online research
  databases and a language-learning platform, accessible with a library
  card login from home or on-site.
