# Accessibility Services

- The library building is wheelchair accessible, with an accessible
  entrance at the front of the building and an elevator to the second
  floor.
- Large-print books and audiobooks are available throughout the
  collection; ask the front desk for the large-print catalogue section.
- A dedicated accessible workstation with screen-reading software is
  available in the Computer Lab; booking is recommended but not required.
- Patrons who are unable to visit in person due to a disability may
  request home delivery of up to 5 items at a time through the
  Homebound Services program; contact the front desk to enroll.
