# Interlibrary Loan

- If an item is not in the Riverbend Community Library catalogue, patrons
  may request it through interlibrary loan from partner library systems
  in the region.
- Requests can be submitted online or at the front desk; typical turnaround
  is 5-10 business days depending on the lending library.
- Interlibrary loan items follow a shorter loan period (2 weeks, no
  renewals) due to lending-library restrictions, and are not eligible for
  the standard overdue fine schedule - a flat $1.00/day fine applies to
  overdue interlibrary loan items, as these affect another library's
  patrons directly.
- There is no fee to request an interlibrary loan, but a lost or damaged
  interlibrary loan item is charged at the lending library's stated
  replacement value.
