# Membership Freeze and Cancellation

- Premium members who will be away for an extended period (60+ days) may
  request a membership freeze; the remaining paid months are added back
  once the membership is reactivated.
- A freeze does not cancel existing loans or holds, which remain subject
  to normal due dates and fines.
- Memberships can be cancelled at any time at the front desk; any
  outstanding fines or unreturned items must be settled before
  cancellation is finalized.
- Premium membership fees are non-refundable once the annual period has
  started, except in cases of relocation outside the service area, which
  may be prorated on request.
