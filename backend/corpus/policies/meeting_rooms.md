# Meeting Room Booking

- The library has 1 large meeting room (capacity 30) available for
  community groups, book clubs, and educational workshops.
- Meeting room booking is free for Premium members and community
  organizations; Standard members may book it for a $10/session fee.
- Bookings must be made at least 3 days in advance through the front
  desk.
- The room includes a projector, whiteboard, and seating for up to 30
  people; audio/visual equipment should be requested at time of booking.
