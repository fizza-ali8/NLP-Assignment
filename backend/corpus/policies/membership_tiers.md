# Membership Tiers

Riverbend Community Library offers two membership tiers.

## Standard Membership (free)
- Borrow up to 5 items at a time
- 14-day loan period (2 weeks)
- 1 active hold at a time
- No annual fee

## Premium Membership ($25/year)
- Borrow up to 15 items at a time
- 21-day loan period (3 weeks)
- Up to 2 active holds at a time
- Extended pickup window (5 days instead of 3) when a held item becomes available
- Free access to the library's meeting rooms (see Meeting Room Booking policy)

Patrons can upgrade from Standard to Premium at any time at the front desk
or through the online account portal. Downgrading takes effect at the next
renewal date.

## Premium vs Standard: what does Premium add?

The difference between Standard and Premium membership, in short: Premium members
can borrow more items at once (15 instead of 5), keep them longer (a 3-week loan
instead of 2 weeks), and place more holds (2 instead of 1). A Premium member also
gets a longer pickup window when a held item arrives (5 days instead of 3) and free
use of the meeting rooms. Premium costs $25 per year; Standard is free.
