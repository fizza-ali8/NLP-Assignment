# Study Room Booking

- The library has 4 private study rooms, each seating up to 6 people.
- Rooms can be booked online or at the front desk up to 7 days in advance.
- Maximum booking length is 2 hours per session; a room may be re-booked
  for an additional 2 hours if no one else has reserved it immediately
  after.
- Bookings are free for both Standard and Premium members.
- A no-show (not checking in within 15 minutes of the booking start time)
  releases the room for walk-in use.
