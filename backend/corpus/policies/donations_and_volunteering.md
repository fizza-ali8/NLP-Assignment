# Donations and Volunteering

## Book donations
- The library accepts gently used book donations during open hours at
  the front desk.
- Donated items are added to the collection, the Friends of the Library
  used-book sale, or a community giveaway shelf, depending on condition
  and current collection needs.
- The library cannot issue tax-donation receipts for individual item
  values but can provide a general donation acknowledgment letter on
  request.

## Volunteering
- Volunteer opportunities include shelving, the Summer Reading Challenge,
  after-school tutoring, and the Friends of the Library book sale.
- Volunteers must be at least 14 years old (or 12 with a parent
  volunteering alongside them) and complete a short orientation session.
