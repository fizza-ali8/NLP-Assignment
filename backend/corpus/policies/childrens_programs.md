# Children's and Youth Programs

- The library runs a weekly Storytime session for ages 2-5, every
  Saturday at 11:00 AM in the Children's Corner.
- A Summer Reading Challenge runs from June through August for school-age
  children, with small prizes for reading milestones.
- After-school homework help is available Monday to Thursday, 3:30 PM -
  5:30 PM, staffed by volunteer tutors; no registration required.
- Children's library cards (for patrons under 13) have the same borrowing
  limits as a Standard membership but no overdue fines - a reminder notice
  is sent instead.
