# Holds and Reservations

- Patrons may place a hold on any item that is currently checked out.
- Standard members may have 1 active hold at a time; Premium members may
  have up to 2 active holds at a time.
- Holds are filled in the order they were placed (first come, first served).
- When a held item becomes available, the patron is notified by email or
  phone and has a pickup window to collect it: 3 days for Standard members,
  5 days for Premium members.
- If the pickup window passes without collection, the hold is cancelled and
  the item moves to the next patron in the queue.
- An item with an active hold cannot be renewed by the patron currently
  holding it.
