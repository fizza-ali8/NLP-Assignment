# Getting a Library Card

- Library cards are free for all residents and students in the service
  area.
- To apply in person, bring a valid photo ID and proof of current address
  (a utility bill, lease agreement, or official mail dated within the
  last 60 days).
- Cards for patrons under 18 require a parent or guardian's signature on
  the application.
- A digital library card is also available through the library's mobile
  app once a physical card has been issued; the digital card can be used
  for checkout at the self-service kiosks.
- Lost cards can be replaced at the front desk for a $2.00 fee; report a
  lost card promptly to avoid unauthorized use.
