# Borrowing and Renewal Policy

## Borrowing
- Standard members may borrow up to 5 items at a time.
- Premium members may borrow up to 15 items at a time.
- Most items have a loan period of 2 weeks (Standard) or 3 weeks (Premium).
- Some popular new-release titles are short-loan items: 1 week (Standard) or
  2 weeks (Premium). The loan period is always shown on the item's catalogue
  record.

## Renewals
- Items may be renewed up to 2 times, as long as no other patron has placed
  a hold on the item.
- Renewals can be done online, by phone, or at the front desk, up to 2 days
  before the due date.
- An item with a hold from another patron cannot be renewed and must be
  returned by its due date.

## Returns
- Items can be returned at the front desk or through the outside book drop,
  which is checked each morning when the library opens.
- Items returned in the book drop after closing are credited to the
  previous day for fine purposes.
