# Overdue Fines and Lost Items

## Overdue fines
- $0.25 per day, per item.
- The maximum fine for any single item is capped at $10.00.
- Fines accrue daily until the item is returned or the cap is reached.
- A patron's account is blocked from new borrowing once their total
  outstanding fines exceed $15.00, until the balance is paid down or
  items are returned.

## Lost or damaged items
- If an item is not returned within 30 days of its due date, it is marked
  lost and the patron is charged the replacement cost of the item plus a
  $5.00 processing fee.
- If a lost item is later found and returned in good condition within 60
  days of being marked lost, the replacement cost is refunded; the $5.00
  processing fee is non-refundable.
- Damaged items (water damage, torn pages, missing components such as a
  DVD case insert) are assessed individually by library staff, who may
  charge a partial or full replacement fee depending on the extent of
  the damage.
