# Library Events and Workshops

- The library hosts a monthly Book Club on the first Tuesday of each
  month at 6:30 PM, open to all members; the current title is posted at
  the front desk and on the library's events board.
- Periodic workshops (resume writing, basic computer skills, tax-season
  help) are announced at least 2 weeks in advance via the library's
  events board and mailing list.
- Event registration, where required, is free and can be done online or
  at the front desk; walk-ins are accommodated as space allows.
