# Printing, Scanning, and Computer Use

- The library has 10 public computers available on a first-come,
  first-served basis, with a 1-hour session limit when others are waiting.
- Black-and-white printing is $0.10/page; color printing is $0.50/page.
- Scanning to a USB drive or email is free.
- Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the building for patrons with their
  own devices; no login is required.
