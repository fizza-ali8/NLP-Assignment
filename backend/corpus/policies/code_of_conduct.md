# Library Code of Conduct

- The library is a shared, quiet space; patrons are asked to keep phone
  calls and conversations to designated areas where possible.
- Food is not permitted near computers or in the stacks; covered
  beverages are allowed throughout the building.
- Children under 8 must be accompanied by a caregiver at all times, except
  during supervised programs such as Storytime.
- Disruptive behavior, including harassment of staff or other patrons,
  may result in a verbal warning, a temporary suspension of library
  privileges, or removal from the premises for repeated violations.
