# Library Hours

- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

## Holiday hours
The library follows a modified holiday schedule around major public
holidays; closures are posted at the front entrance and on the library's
notice board at least one week in advance. The library is closed on New
Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

## Book drop
The outside book drop is accessible 24 hours a day, including when the
library is closed.
